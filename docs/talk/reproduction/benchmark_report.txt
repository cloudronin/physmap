Redistribution basis -- read this before reusing any of this data

  2 of the 7 shipped datasets carry affirmative permission: public domain or an open licence.
  5 ship WITHOUT a licence: forrest, casper_hypersonic_transition, marineau_hypersonic_transition, dirker_water, jin_sco2_buoyancy.
  Shipping is not licensing. They rest on the position that measured values
  are facts.
  4 of them are published AGAINST an express publisher
  term rather than under one: forrest, casper_hypersonic_transition, dirker_water, jin_sco2_buoyancy.
  Only the numbers are redistributed; no paper, figure or PDF. They are
  removed on objection -- see NOTICE.

Data quality -- separate from redistribution, and not implied by it

  Not benchmark-grade by their own account: forrest.
  A dataset can be perfectly legal to publish and still be unfit to
  benchmark on. Their cells are reported so the weakness is
  inspectable, not so it can be cited.

  forrest
    source      Forrest et al., J. Heat Transfer 138(2):021704
    values      Visual estimates from Fig 5 of the version of record
    ships       yes (against_publisher_terms)
    licensed    NO
    QUALITY     TRIAGE_ONLY -- The file's own header: 'VISUAL ESTIMATES ... NOT digitized by WebPlotDigitizer. Use ONLY as a resolvability triage check.' Its benchmark cell is also degenerate -- 1 training row, no detector fit -- so its DO_NO_HARM outcome is short-circuited rather than earned. Published so both weaknesses are visible; re-digitise Fig 5 properly before treating this cell as evidence.
    basis       ASME holds copyright and requires written permission to reproduce. The CC-BY route does not apply: ASME grants it for accepted manuscripts in INSTITUTIONAL repositories, the OSTI deposit is a FUNDER repository, and the deposited PDF carries no Creative Commons marking at all. Shipped anyway, as an accepted risk. Numbers only.

  casper_hypersonic_transition
    source      Casper MS thesis (DTIC ADA504177) and AIAA 2009-4054
    values      Figure digitisation, 600-DPI segmentation, two readers
    ships       yes (against_publisher_terms)
    licensed    NO
    basis       The rows carrying the result come from the AIAA paper, and AIAA prohibits using their content to develop machine-learning models without written permission -- which is what a public ML benchmark is. Shipped anyway, as an accepted risk. Weaker than Marineau, not equal to it: the OSTI copy carries no copyright notice AND no public-release marking, just silence. Numbers only.

  marineau_hypersonic_transition
    source      Marineau et al., AIAA 2014-3108 / SAND2014-4326C
    values      Transcribed from Table 3
    ships       yes (no_licence_facts_basis)

    basis       No reuse licence exists, and no prohibition either. The OSTI copy carries no copyright notice and is marked public-release-unlimited by the controlling office -- a security determination, not a licence, and OSTI expressly disclaims granting one. Ships on the position that measured values transcribed from a published table are facts. Government-funded, government-hosted, transcribed rather than digitised: the cleanest of the unlicensed three.

  dirker_water
